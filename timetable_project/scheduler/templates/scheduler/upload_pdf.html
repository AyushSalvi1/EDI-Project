What the importer understands

Academic calendar

  • Semester / session name and dates (03 Aug 2026, 2026-08-03, 03/08/2026)
  • Working days, including ranges like Monday to Friday
  • College timings, including 12-hour forms like 09:00 am to 05:00 pm
  • Period duration and lunch break

Teaching grid

  • Teacher, subject, year, division and semester hours in any column order
  • Division strength and per-teacher weekly limits
  • Lab subjects marked Lab, Practical, P or Theory
  • Teacher unavailability such as MON-P2 or Monday Period 2
  • Tables that continue across page breaks are rejoined automatically
